# Spec: Provisional top navigation model

## Status

Draft for review

## Purpose

Propose a provisional top navigation model for the unified Health Store and Global Store experience.

## Research direction

Current evidence supports a subject-first IA with task, format, audience, and merchandising overlays.

Persona and role matter, but Phase 1 evidence does not strongly support persona-first navigation as the primary IA.

## Proposed top navigation

| Navigation item | Role | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Browse by Subject | Primary | Supports subject, specialty, and discipline-led browsing |
| Learning & Exam Prep | Primary / task | Supports exam, certification, revision, student learning, and clinical skills needs |
| Books, Journals & Reference | Primary / format | Supports content-type and product-format browsing |
| Clinical Skills, Diagnostics & Procedures | Primary or secondary | Supports diagnostic, procedural, and clinical technique discovery |
| For Your Role | Secondary / persona | Supports audience pathways without making persona the whole IA |
| Featured & New | Merchandising | Supports bestsellers, new releases, featured collections, and campaigns |
